🍛 Bihar's Iconic Foods

🫓

Litti Chokha

Bihar's soul food — roasted wheat balls stuffed with sattu, served with smoky baigan chokha and drowned in desi ghee.

🥤

Sattu Sharbat

The original protein shake — refreshing cold drink made from roasted gram flour with lemon and spices.

🍬

Khaja

GI-tagged layered crispy pastry from Silao, soaked in sugar syrup — a 2,500-year-old recipe!

🍚

Dahi Chura

Flattened rice with thick curd and jaggery — the ceremonial Bihari breakfast during festivals.

🍢

Bihari Kebab

Tender minced meat kebabs marinated with poppy seed paste — Patna's legendary street food.

🍪

Tilkut

Crunchy sesame and sugar sweet from Gaya — a winter delicacy especially during Makar Sankranti.

🎭 Folk Arts & Traditions

🎨
Madhubani Painting
Ancient Mithila art with intricate geometric patterns using natural dyes. UNESCO recognized.
💃
Jat-Jatin
Traditional folk dance depicting love between husband and wife during monsoon season.
🎵
Bidesia
Folk theatre by Bhikhari Thakur — the "Shakespeare of Bihar" — about migration and love.
🪘
Jhijhiya
Women's folk dance performed during Durga Puja, invoking blessings and celebrating womanhood.
🎶
Nirgun-Nirgunia
Devotional folk music tradition exploring themes of spirituality and detachment.
🏺
Sujni Embroidery
Traditional quilting art from Bhusura village depicting social narratives and daily life.

🪔 Cultural Celebrations

🪔 Chhath Puja

Bihar's most sacred festival dedicated to the Sun God. Devotees stand in water offering prayers at sunset and sunrise. Celebrated across all districts with spectacular ghats.

🐘 Sonepur Mela

Asia's largest cattle fair held at the confluence of Gandak and Ganga rivers — a month-long cultural extravaganza with folk performances.

📜 Maithili Literature

One of India's 22 scheduled languages, with rich literary tradition dating back to Vidyapati's immortal poetry on love and devotion.

🎪 Rajgir Mahotsav

Annual cultural festival in the ancient city of Rajgir featuring classical dance, music, and art performances against historical backdrops.
